# Main figure and table plan

The revised article uses a restrained grayscale visual style. Legacy exploratory figures remain in the replication package but are not in the main article.

## Figure 1. Relief, yield, retention, and the two mechanisms
`figures/figure1_conceptual.{png,pdf}`. Constraint displacement affects relief-to-yield; requirement expansion affects yield-to-retention.

## Figure 2. Supported versus retained capability relative to matched no aid
`figures/figure2_supported_retained.{png,pdf}` from `stage4_control_matched_capability_v1.csv`. Quadrants distinguish success-without-retention, both-positive, and both-negative regimes.

## Figure 3. Requirement-expansion evidence
`figures/figure3_requirement_expansion.{png,pdf}`. Panel A shows the frozen-telemetry decomposition. Panel B shows +30-day composite-capability branch effects in normal and demand-clamped fresh-seed arms; an annotation reports that the coverage branch gap is zero in all pairs despite a successful demand manipulation.

## Figure 4. Capability trajectories by benchmark
`figures/figure4_capability_trajectories.{png,pdf}`. Supported effect vs no aid, retained effect vs no aid, and dependency gap across all five horizons. Shading is interquartile range across cells, not a sampling-confidence interval.

## Figure 5. Logistics capability-retention-cost frontier
`figures/figure5_assistance_frontier.{png,pdf}`. Substitution, development, and hybrid arms with marker area scaled to modeled cumulative donor cost.
