# Submission target and manuscript-shaping notes

Current primary target: **Security Studies**

Status: working target for Draft 0.5. This is not a submission decision that
prevents later retargeting.

## Why the current manuscript fits

The article asks an analytical security-studies question rather than advocating
a specific security-assistance policy. Its contribution is a general theory of
how successful assistance changes military production constraints, tested with
a prospectively frozen synthetic experiment and structured historical process
evidence.

The journal's current author guidance explicitly welcomes methodological
diversity and expects research articles to answer explanatory puzzles, develop a
clear theoretical argument, use rigorous substantive analysis, engage work
beyond the immediate subfield, and spell out implications for further research.
That is the architecture Draft 0.5 is being built to satisfy.

## Length target

Security Studies normally caps research articles at 13,000 words excluding
online-only supplementary information, while allowing review of manuscripts up
to 15,000 words for rich qualitative research. Draft 0.5 deliberately carries
some material that can move to the online supplement. After Stage-5 results are
inserted, the main-text compression pass should remove duplicated methods,
robustness, and historical detail while preserving the full novelty boundary and
headline evidence.

## Main-text material to preserve

1. the puzzle and novelty boundary;
2. relief, yield, retention, and constraint migration theory;
3. formal growth-versus-demand result in accessible form;
4. Stage-4 research design and provenance;
5. autonomy-trap, migration/yield, and substitution/development results;
6. concise robustness evidence;
7. Stage-5 prospective design and eventual headline results;
8. compact four-case historical process evidence;
9. scope conditions, falsifiers, and limitations;
10. data and reproducibility statement.

## Material to move to online supplement

- complete Stage-4 cell tables and seed distributions;
- full smooth-aggregator results;
- exact cryptographic artifact manifests;
- full Lean theorem statements and axiom audit;
- complete Stage-5 structure-by-intensity tables;
- full historical coding sheets and source notes;
- extended model implementation details;
- any structural-falsification sidecar experiment;
- exhaustive reproduction commands.

## Alternative targets

**International Studies Quarterly** is a plausible retarget if the final paper
successfully generalizes beyond security assistance to a broader theory of
externally enabled organizational capacity. Its current research-article limit
is 12,000 words including tables, figures, footnotes, and bibliography, so an
ISQ version would require tighter compression.

**Journal of Conflict Resolution** is plausible if the final contribution is
presented primarily as a general formal/computational theory of conflict-related
capacity building. Its current total limit is approximately 8,000 to 11,000
words, which would require moving more theory and historical detail to
supplementary material.

An **International Security** submission can be reconsidered after Stage 5 and
the literature intervention if the findings support a sufficiently broad and
important security-policy theory. The current manuscript should not be inflated
or rhetorically overstated merely to pursue that venue.

## Submission-stage transparency requirements

The final package should include:

- an anonymized main manuscript;
- a separate author/title page;
- exact word count;
- data-availability statement;
- replication README connecting every paper table and figure to source data and
  code;
- public or review-accessible synthetic evidence package after repository
  history sanitization;
- clear disclosure separating original Stage 4, post-completion synthesis,
  prospective Stage 5, and post-Stage-4 historical validation.
