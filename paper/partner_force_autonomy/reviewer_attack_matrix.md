# Hostile-review matrix

This file is an internal pre-submission red-team checklist. The objective is to
make the manuscript answer predictable reviewer objections before submission,
not to suppress or rhetorically evade them.

| Reviewer objection | Current answer in Draft 0.4 | Remaining vulnerability | Required action before submission |
|---|---|---|---|
| "This is just the old claim that aid creates dependence." | The paper explicitly concedes dependency, sustainment, absorptive-capacity, and premature-load-bearing arguments as prior literature. Contribution is relief-yield-retention divergence, support-enabled demand, constraint migration, headroom, and local-system divergence. | Full literature intervention could still uncover a direct predecessor. | Run adversarial deep literature review across SFA, state capacity, operations research, organizational complementarity, and logistics. Narrow novelty if an equivalent mechanism exists. |
| "You programmed the result into a hard minimum." | Alternative arithmetic, geometric, and harmonic feasibility measures agree in sign with the hard minimum in 167/169 observed-matched Migration worlds. | This robustness is strongest for sign, not every effect magnitude or every module. | Keep claim limited and place full alternative-aggregator results in supplement. |
| "Logistics wins because the model architecture makes logistics a sink." | Manuscript explicitly labels logistics persistence as model-specific and not a general law. Stage 5 varies development breadth, and structural falsification remains separately documented. | A reviewer may still argue the main migration result is architecture-dependent. | Complete one compact structural falsification if existing evidence cannot demonstrate that alternative terminal constraints are possible. Report failure if it occurs. |
| "Bottleneck migration does not mean the intervention mattered." | Draft 0.4 separates constraint relief from system yield. Force generation migrates 46/46 while mean +30-day composite gain is approximately zero; logistics has 0/85 migration but larger supported yield. | Headroom itself has not yet been estimated world-by-world from frozen trajectories. | Derive a reproducible headroom/yield analysis from existing telemetry if fields permit. Label as post-completion synthesis. |
| "The model merely proves that complementary inputs are complementary." | The paper anchors complementarity in prior organizational theory and makes the empirical contribution about endogenous constraint identity, induced demand, and retained partner-owned capability. | Stage-4 single-channel results alone cannot show whether coordinated growth escapes the trap. | Use prospectively frozen Stage 5 as the direct rising-tide test. Do not change hypotheses after results. |
| "The cases were selected after you saw the result." | The manuscript discloses that Stage-4 results and broad case familiarity preceded historical coding. Case set, coding fields, source hierarchy, rival requirement, and disconfirmation rule were frozen before systematic coding. | Four cases are still a purposive validation set, not an identified sample. | Present historical evidence only as plausibility/process support/cross-case consistency, never population causal inference. |
| "Your historical cases cherry-pick only successes." | Mali is coded as a hard mixed case and explicitly weakens a clean migration claim; Colombia is a positive retention case; Afghanistan emphasizes persistence rather than migration. | Source audit remains preliminary. | Finish source audit, preserve adverse evidence, report ambiguous cells in final matrix. |
| "Afghanistan or Iraq had political failures, so your technical mechanism is irrelevant." | Political explanations are treated as coexisting mechanisms and recorded as rivals. The theory is conditional on real service-production changes occurring. | Could still sound technocratic if historical discussion gets too causal. | Keep process claims narrow and avoid explaining whole-country collapse with production constraints. |
| "Synthetic effect sizes have no external meaning." | Manuscript repeatedly states thresholds and numerical effects are synthetic-model quantities, not historical estimates. | Policy prose could accidentally imply calibrated real-world magnitudes. | Keep policy implications at the level of what to measure and how to diagnose, not numerical prescriptions. |
| "The paper is post hoc." | Stage 4 was prospectively frozen; the relief-yield-retention reframing and weighted synthesis are explicitly labeled post-completion; Stage 5 is separately prospectively frozen before outcomes. | Readers may conflate stages. | Maintain provenance labels in abstract/methods/results/disclosure and supplement. |
| "Force-generation development rising while system autonomy stays zero is trivial under the metric." | It is used as evidence of local-system divergence and survives the broader conceptual distinction between local output and system feasibility. | Need show it is not merely an artifact of a knife-edge minimum. | Report smooth-aggregator local-system robustness if derivable; otherwise limit the exact zero claim to the formal system coordinate and focus on the qualitative divergence. |
| "What would falsify the theory?" | Migration need not occur universally; broad development is predicted to help near ties more than dominant-bottleneck systems; positive retention is possible when indigenous production catches up. | Falsifiers are distributed across text rather than summarized. | Add an explicit falsifiable-predictions paragraph or table before submission. |
| "So what should policy makers do?" | Measure relief, headroom, system yield, post-relief constraint, and retention rather than only assisted-subsystem output. | No validated optimal controller or allocation rule. | Keep recommendations diagnostic. Present adaptive allocation as future testing, not proven policy. |

## Submission gate

Do not call the paper submission-ready until all of the following are true:

1. Stage-5 frozen analysis is complete and either integrated or reported as a
   null/adverse result without redesigning the test.
2. Historical source audit is complete for all four cases.
3. The adversarial literature intervention is complete.
4. Any necessary structural falsification is resolved.
5. Headroom is either directly analyzed from frozen telemetry or clearly kept as
   a theoretical diagnostic only.
6. Main text and supplement pass the evidence validator and no-em-dash check.
7. Git history is sanitized before any public replication release.
