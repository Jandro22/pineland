# Pineland General Theory v1

This directory is a synthetic theory-extraction program layered on the current Pineland scientific core. It does not modify historical parameters and does not authorize a Nepal/Afghanistan rerun.

The objective is explanatory compression: determine whether the full ABM can be reduced to a small conditional theory of competitive local reproduction, then falsify that reduction before historical transport.

Core contracts:
- `general_theory_contract_v1.json`: substantive candidate and promotion firewall.
- `model_to_theory_map_v1.json`: audited Rust-state/process to theory mapping.
- `rival_reproduction_architectures_v1.json`: OR/MAX/additive/complementary/bottleneck/threshold rival family.
- `macrostate_closure_contract_v1.json`: state sufficiency and coarse-graining tests.
- `multitype_reproduction_operator_contract_v1.json`: endogenous reproduction accounting and criticality.
- `dimensionless_groups_v1.json`: candidate timescale/capacity groups and phase regimes.
- `competitive_reproduction_contract_v1.json`: state-side reproduction extension.
- `experiment_registry_v1.json`: dependency order.

Scientific firewall: synthetic experiments may select or refute theory structures. Historical outcomes may confront a frozen theory but may not choose mechanisms, functional forms, or parameters.
