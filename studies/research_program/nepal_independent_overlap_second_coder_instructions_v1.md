# Nepal independent-overlap second-coder protocol v1

Work only from `nepal_independent_overlap_second_coder_packet_v1.csv` and its linked public sources. Do not inspect the existing Nepal control/presence staging, adjudicated evidence, blind keys, agreement outputs, model outputs, or predictive results.

For each row, independently decide whether the linked source supports a dated, actor-specific control/presence observable in the target district and sampling window. The target district/window is a sampling cell, not a required answer.

Allowed observables remain: `government_armed_presence`, `maoist_armed_presence`, `government_administrative_presence`, `maoist_shadow_governance`, `checkpoint_or_patrol_presence`, `security_post_or_garrison_presence`, `territorial_access_constraint`, `territorial_control_claim`. If the source does not support an admissible observation, use `NOT_ADMISSIBLE`.

Do not infer control from violence, fatalities, attack occurrence, source silence, or presumed political support. Preserve the narrowest directly supported spatial and temporal scope. Do not carry an observation outside its stated interval. Fill every coder field and use HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW confidence with a short rationale.

Do not decide whether the source is independent of another source; source-genealogy adjudication occurs only after blind coding is locked.
