# Empirical status after the first Afghanistan transfer

Pineland remains a verified experimental apparatus, not a validated predictive
model of insurgency. Nepal is a preserved falsification and the completed
Afghanistan 5,036-day experiment is a failed first transfer under its frozen v1
measurement rule.

## Afghanistan result

All three strength trajectories reached day 5,036. They generated nonzero
latent and recorded contacts, but held-out province-week sensitivity remained
near zero and every statistical violence competitor dominated the Pineland
strength mixture on every holdout. The October 2017 scalar control comparison
had MAE around 0.226 and zero/negative district correlations.

The old absolute supply threshold incorrectly failed residuals around
4.3–4.9e-5 after hundreds of millions of supply units of flow. The derived
assessment now uses `atol + rtol * gross_scale`; it passes these runs as
numerically conserved without changing outcomes.

## Measurement boundary

`ControlVector.effective()` remains an internal dashboard aggregate. It is not
a SIGAR observation operator. New Afghanistan runs retain both actors' seven
components and use a prospectively declared ordinal probability model. The
operator passed synthetic category recovery without historical fitting. The
legacy v1 target snapshots cannot be rescored because their components were
not retained.

## Spatial diagnosis

At province-week scale, the historical panel has one-week persistence 0.639
and adjacent-province activation 0.281. The three trajectories have persistence
0, 0, and 0.056 and adjacent activation 0 in all cases. This supports a focused
synthetic program on local persistence and connected propagation; it does not
license changing contact rate to improve Afghanistan.
