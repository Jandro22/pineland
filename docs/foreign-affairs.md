# Phase 8 foreign powers and internationalization

Phase 8 adds five heterogeneous neighboring states around landlocked Pineland.
They have independent preferences for stability, alignment, ideology, border
security, influence, commerce, humanitarian outcomes, and cost avoidance. Their
willingness is driven by their own imperfect beliefs, costs, casualties,
domestic opposition, strategic value, and rival activity—not host-government
utility.

## Borders, migration, and diaspora

Each Pineland district has a first-class border segment with physical terrain,
infrastructure, legal permeability, social permeability, language overlap,
kinship overlap, and monitoring. Legal and social permeability are deliberately
independent.

Residents can undertake temporary flight, refuge, or economic migration and
later return. They remain Person agents, keep their Pineland residence and
social adjacency for return/reference, and acquire an external-state marker and
a decaying origin-tie strength. Diaspora links carry conserved remittances and
imperfect political information back through those persistent relationships.

## Support and sanctuary

External support is an eight-component vector: financial, political, material,
training, organizational, diplomatic, sanctuary, and direct. Each component
has a separate destination effect. Every component is also an explicit
external-transfer record. Government support can finance the state, transfer
institutional learning, and influence legitimacy. Insurgent support changes
resources, formation quality/supply, organizational capital, sponsor
dependence, phenotype, and mechanistic sanctuary.

Sanctuary lowers organizational collapse exposure but does not remove distance,
command, logistics, or local-embeddedness costs. Sponsor and client preferences
remain distinct.

## Intervention, knowledge, and withdrawal

Direct interveners instantiate ordinary `Organization`, `ArmedFormation`,
`SupplySource`, `SecurityPost`, and `Patrol` objects. They use the Phase 3
movement/logistics engine, Phase 4 observations, and Phase 5 contact resolver.
Their low initial embeddedness and language knowledge create an epistemic
disadvantage.

Foreign beliefs are noisy transformations of host beliefs, never ground truth.
Interpreter-brokers are real socially central Pineland residents. Their two-way
language, trust, and cultural knowledge improve confidence without creating
perfect information.

Foreign presence produces heterogeneous legitimacy effects through local
identity, language, trust, security benefit, and harm. Assistance can build host
capacity or crowd it out. Diagnostics distinguish host capacity, foreign
capacity, dependence, absorption, withdrawal speed, and withdrawal shock.

Foreign willingness updates on a slow monthly clock. When it falls below the
configured threshold, formations receive ordinary movement orders to their
border locality and become external only after arriving; they are never deleted
as an instantaneous withdrawal shortcut. Rival interventions raise other
states' incentives and produce an externalization-reproduction diagnostic.

The production constitutional election interval is 1,460 days (four years).
Accelerated election calendars belong only in explicit test scenarios.

## Outputs

`foreign_diagnostics.json` reports beliefs, dependence, intervention,
externalization, and diaspora measures. `external_support.jsonl` and
`external_transfers.jsonl` preserve typed support and conserved resource flows.
The controlled A/B/C experiment compares no intervention, foreign substitution,
and capacity building under equal foreign force capacity followed by withdrawal.
