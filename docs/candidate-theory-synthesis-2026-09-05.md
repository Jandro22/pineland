# Candidate theory synthesis (2026-09-05)

## Scientific status

The strongest current result is a falsifiable candidate, not a stable general
theory. The proposed mechanism is **competitive local reproduction**: an
insurgency persists when local access, information, mobilization, armed
presence, control, civilian expectations, and future access form a renewing
cycle. Counterinsurgency succeeds when persistent, information-rich, locally
credible security and governance interrupt that cycle faster than insurgents
can renew or relocate it.

The unit of explanation is therefore viable local reproduction—not national
manpower or violence totals alone. Manpower matters through fielding,
sustainment, local presence, and effective control.

No general-theory or COIN-efficacy claim is licensed. Nepal remains a
falsification; Afghanistan requires revalidation against the current core;
future cases remain data-construction stages.

## Narrowed surviving conjecture

The strongest statement that survives the current measurement checks is weaker
than the original theory: local renewal is a potentially useful predictor of a
case-defined event process only when the event stratum and location-assignment
rule are held fixed. It is not yet evidence of actor-level reproduction, and
adjacency is not yet a temporally directed propagation law. Any stronger claim
must survive stratum-specific, assignment-aware, actor-attributed, and
control/joint-outcome holdouts.

## Evidence that motivates the candidate

- Nepal's residual funnel identifies contact opportunity/hazard as a dominant
  activity bottleneck, while the post-repair process still underproduces the
  historical activity shape.
- Nepal ablations show that formation scale, reserve coverage, adaptive
  response, and logistics change activity and spatial concentration, but the
  existing results are descriptive ablations rather than causal estimates.
- Synthetic diagnostics show that insurgent movement is a mixed
  frontier/foothold/stronghold/exploration policy. Government gap-filling is
  not consistently identifiable because some matched scenarios produce no
  eligible reallocation order. In runs where the diagnostic is eligible,
  government patrols write decaying presence memory while ordinary stationary
  insurgent formation occupancy does not.
- Afghanistan's archived transfer diagnostic shows near-zero renewal and
  neighbor activation in the frozen target ensemble, together with weak
  district control ranking and a large manpower-to-control gap.

These observations support investigation of local reproduction, but they do
not distinguish persistence, relocation, recruitment, contact hazard, and
observation error well enough to promote any mechanism.

The bounded factorial adds a useful negative constraint. Geographic
restriction can change renewal and spatial concentration even when it produces
no adjacent activations, while symmetric formation memory can be exactly null
when the contact pathway is unexercised. Thus adjacency and static presence
memory are not sufficient mechanisms. The candidate theory should be written
conjunctively: local reproduction requires a viable exposure/contact pathway,
an initiation or recruitment response, and enough local sustainment to renew
the episode. Movement and presence can shape those terms, but cannot substitute
for them. This is a falsifiable refinement, not a general-theory result.

The data-free spatial identification battery now passes all eleven recovery
gates, including known propagation, persistence, dose response, topology
placebos, and spatial-frailty/degree/common-shock nulls. This licenses the
estimands and diagnostics—not a claim that the historical model reproduces
them.

The prospective SIGAR observation operator also passes its synthetic recovery
gate (99.24% category accuracy at 2,500 generated observations) without using
the October 2017 target. This validates the ordinal measurement contract, not
the latent-control dynamics or historical transfer.

A fresh 14-day, 300-agent live-simulator genealogy pilot produced 21
activation episodes: 18 initial conditions, one relocation, one local force
generation, and one local recruitment episode. Only one parent edge was
available, so historical `R_I` remains unidentified.

The bounded four-cell factorial pilot now executes all preregistered
study-layer treatments with a stable live-source hash and exact supply

all cells had zero latent contacts and zero adjacent activations, while the
memory treatment was indistinguishable from its matched patrol-only counterpart
through both the 14-day pilot and a one-seed 42-day extension. Neighbor
restriction changed renewal and Moran's I in the extension, but without an
activation pathway or synthetic truth recovery this is not a causal factor
claim. The experiment remains below the preregistered acceptance gate.

An observation-only event-panel screen now adds a useful cross-case constraint
without touching the simulator core. On the complete GADM-unit by observed-
month violence grids, one-month same-unit renewal was 0.267 in Colombia and
0.501 in Iraq. Adjacency-conditioned future activation was 0.091 and 0.080,
respectively; valid endpoint-relabeling topology nulls were 0.046 and 0.045,
respectively. The corrected reverse-time placebo is 0.090 and 0.079, respectively;
the harmonized four-case screen is mixed (Nepal 0.352 vs 0.361 placebo,
Afghanistan 0.134 vs 0.133, Colombia 0.091 vs 0.090, Iraq 0.080 vs 0.079),
with only a negligible pooled margin. The descriptive result therefore
supports spatial clustering as a measurable pattern, but not a robust
parent-attributed propagation mechanism. Missing actor identity,
control/presence joins, and case-specific observation models keep these cells
outside theory promotion.
One-month case-native stratum sensitivity makes the measurement warning

and Taliban one-sided renewal about 0.09. These are not interchangeable
insurgent outcomes, so the pooled signature cannot carry a stronger claim than

A training-only simple-competitor screen adds a second cross-case constraint.
On deterministic temporal holdouts, the local-renewal feature improved Brier
score over a global baseline in all four cases: Nepal +0.069, Afghanistan
+0.020, Colombia +0.0004, and Iraq +0.0148. The local-plus-neighbor model was
best in each case, but the neighbor-only lift was small in Colombia and Iraq
(+0.0001 and +0.0008). These are predictive violence associations on an
exploratory split—not identified insurgent reproduction, control, or a causal
advantage—and Nepal/Afghanistan still require current-core impact assessment.
Three blocked rolling-origin sensitivity windows (50%, 60%, and 70% prefixes)
keep local renewal positive in every case and window, while the combined model
is positive in all windows but adds little beyond renewal in several cases.
Nepal's weekly rows are aggregated to months, and location assignment and
case-native event definitions remain heterogeneous, so these windows are
chronology checks rather than preregistered holdout evidence.
Stratum-level competitor checks preserve an important negative result:
Afghanistan's Taliban state-based events retain positive local lift, but the
faction, government one-sided, and external strata are slightly negative. The
pooled predictor advantage is therefore not stable across event definitions.
The complete record is

## Blocking questions

1. Is low activity caused by contact hazard, latent local reproduction, or the
   observation operator?
2. Why does mobilized or fielded manpower produce so little effective local
   control?
3. How much apparent activation is true parent-attributed reproduction versus
   relocation, initial conditions, or unlinked local generation?
4. Does geographic adjacency, rather than national destination choice, drive
   diffusion?
5. Does the control observation operator recover historical categories from
   the seven-dimensional latent state?

## Most efficient next experiment

The synthetic pathway replication is complete. The next efficient experiment
is a superseding-core revalidation: classify and repair the remaining live-core
drift, issue a new reproducibility certificate, and only then rerun the
current-core Nepal/Afghanistan transfer against the preserved zero-contact and
legacy failed-transfer baselines. Do not use the eight-seed synthetic artifact
as a substitute for that historical impact assessment.

The revalidation should retain the same matched-seed 2x2 factors:

| Factor | Level 1 | Level 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Presence memory | government patrol-only | symmetric stationary-formation memory |
| Destination choice | current global locality choice | geographic-neighbor-restricted choice |

Retain predeclared renewal, adjacent activation, parent-attributed
reproduction, Moran's I, Gini, active cells, event totals, movement, manpower,
and supply conservation. The memory factor should primarily move renewal and
temporal clustering; the geographic factor should primarily move adjacent
activation and Moran's I. Require content-addressed provenance and a stable
superseding certificate before interpreting any transfer result.
