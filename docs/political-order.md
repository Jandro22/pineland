# Phase 7 political order

Phase 7 models the state, incumbent government, and political parties as
distinct objects. Citizens separately hold state legitimacy, government
legitimacy, party legitimacy, and access to peaceful political channels.

## Institutional ecosystem

The generated federal ecosystem contains executive, legislature, civil
administration, judiciary, military, and police institutions. Every district
has a district government and every locality a municipal administration. Each
institution has slow-moving capacity, autonomy, compliance, reach, integrity,
resources, and governing-party influence. Identical federal allocations can
therefore be implemented, delayed, distorted, or diverted differently locally.

Political parties have explicit locality branches, overlapping membership,
resources, patronage stocks, electoral support, institutional influence, and
elite brokers. Local elites are selected from socially central residents and
carry resources, legitimacy, institutional ties, and political alignment.

## Fiscal and governance flow

Each political cycle conservatively divides a federal policy budget among
public provision, patronage, and private diversion. Local noncompliance further
redirects intended public spending into patronage and private stocks. Transfers
are explicit records rather than scalar corruption penalties.

Public governance produces security, justice, administration, services, and
representation from budget, capacity, reach, compliance, integrity, autonomy,
and local infrastructure. Capacity learns slowly from successful production
and decays through poor implementation and patronage. Combat deployment remains
much faster than institutional reconstruction.

Governance experience updates citizen legitimacy and political access. Policy
may alter administrative, legal, social, or expected control through the causal
ledger, but neither policy nor elections write physical control.

## Elections and conflict ecology

Turnout depends on political access and state legitimacy. Party choice depends
on personal preference and legitimacy plus the local branch's support and
patronage. Elections change the ruling party and measurable institutional
influence while leaving state legitimacy and locality control intact at the
instant of election.

Political access raises the utility of party participation, civil society, and
protest while lowering armed-participation utility. The same access reduces
Phase 6 proto-organization formation and recruitment hazards. Governance can
therefore redirect grievance into peaceful contention without deleting an
armed organization.

## Outputs

`political_diagnostics.json` summarizes legitimacy, access, capacity, party
influence, spending, and diversion. `political_transfers.jsonl`,
`policy_implementations.jsonl`, and `elections.jsonl` preserve auditable fiscal,
implementation, and electoral histories. Political/onset scale sensitivity is
available as an ensemble distribution diagnostic rather than a single paired
onset trajectory.
